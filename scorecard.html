B-BBEE · Skills Development

What the academy does for the scorecard

Training is not only something SPS gives its people — it is a measured element of the company’s B-BBEE scorecard, and it is the element the academy exists inside. This page is the Skills Development element as it came back from verification, with every indicator explained in plain language and linked to the part of the academy that feeds it.

These figures are the verification report’s, transcribed. The academy does not calculate B-BBEE and none of the numbers here are its own working. The report itself is not published on this site — it carries company registration, VAT and ownership detail that has no business on a web page. Read the notes at the foot of this page before quoting anything off it.

Reading this without getting it wrong

One element is broken down here, not five.
B-BBEE is scored across five elements and the Level 2 comes from all of them together. Skills Development is the one the academy sits inside, so it is the only one this page opens up. The other four appear as totals and nothing more — the academy has no business explaining a procurement scorecard.
It is a closed period, and it expires.
Everything here measures 01 March 2025 to 28 February 2026, verified 07 July 2026, on the Amended Construction Sector Codes. The certificate expires 02 August 2027. Targets differ between sector codes, so none of these numbers transfer to a company measured on another one.
The totals are added up, not re-scored.
Two things on this page are calculated rather than transcribed: the twelve scores added together, and how far each line fell short of its own weighting. Both were checked back against the report — the scores sum to 24.63 exactly, and every line follows the same formula to the cent. That is arithmetic on the report’s figures, not a second opinion about them.
A course’s Learning Programme Matrix category is the practitioner’s call.
Categories A to G decide what a programme is worth on two of these indicators, and assigning them is the B-BBEE practitioner’s job. This page says what kind of activity counts on a line; it never claims a particular course is a Category B or C.
Enrolling is not a B-BBEE transaction.
Courses are fully funded because SPS decided to fund them. That the spend also counts on this element is true, and it is why the page exists — but it is not the reason to do a course, and nobody should be enrolled to move a number.
