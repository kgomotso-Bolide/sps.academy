Skills Gap Analysis

Find out what you're missing — in about two minutes

Answer a few questions about your role and rate yourself honestly. We'll compare that against what the job actually asks for, show you where the gaps are, and point you at the courses that close them fastest.

Before you start This runs entirely on your own device. Your answers are never uploaded, never stored, and nobody at SPS sees them — not HR, not your manager. Close the tab and they're gone. If you want to share the result, print it and take it to your next one-on-one.
How it works

No black box

Worth knowing what's actually happening, so you can judge the result rather than just trust it.

1. A target per role

Every role has a target level across eight skill areas — a solar installer is expected to be strong on technical systems, someone in sales on communication and reporting. Your gap is simply the distance between your rating and that target.

2. Courses scored against your gaps

Each course is mapped to the skills it moves. A course scores higher when it lands on the areas where your gap is biggest, so the ranking is about you rather than about what's popular.

3. Experience changes the route

Your qualification and years on the job don't change what's missing — they change how you close it. Years on the tools with nothing on paper points to the full qualification rather than another short course, and if your experience has run well ahead of your certificates the result will flag Recognition of Prior Learning as a route worth asking about.

Two honest caveats. The role targets are a first pass, written from how this site describes the work — they haven't yet been signed off by the people who actually run these teams, so treat the result as a conversation-starter for your next one-on-one rather than a verdict on your career. And the recommendations currently draw only on the courses live on this site; our catalogue now runs to 800 qualifications and courses, so if your gap is in something we don't yet list, ask HR — there is very likely a course for it.
