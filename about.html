About SPS Academy

Investing Our Profits, In Our People

SPS Academy is our own training academy, for our own people. Every course is paid for by the company and runs online or in person.

We started with AI. The catalogue now covers technical, business, compliance, safety and admin — so whichever part of SPS you work in, there is something aimed at your job.

  • No cost to you — every course is funded by the company
  • Online or in person, around your shift
  • Accredited qualifications for anyone who wants to take it further
  • Skills mapped to the work we actually do here, not abstract theory
What that means day to day

Installers, field teams, sales and support — practical courses that fit around the working day, and a qualification at the end of it for anyone who wants one.
