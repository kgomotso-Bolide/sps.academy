About SPS Academy

Investing Our Profits, In Our People

SPS offers training in strategic leadership, technical and digital fluency, change management, and navigating organisational dynamics during periods of transformation. This is complemented by mentorship with senior leaders and technical experts, who provide guidance on career progression, help employees build resilience in the face of technological change, and advocate for their mentees' professional growth. These initiatives aren't just about individual development, they're about strengthening our talent pipeline as a whole, so that as the industry changes, our people change and grow with it.

We started with AI. As of August 2026 the catalogue runs to 800 qualifications and courses — technical, business, compliance, safety and admin — so whichever part of SPS you work in, there is something here aimed at your actual job.

  • A catalogue of 800 qualifications and courses, reaching well beyond AI
  • A practical understanding of how AI tools work and where they help in your role
  • The confidence to use AI responsibly and effectively in your day-to-day work
  • Formal qualifications for the people who want to take it further
  • Skills mapped to the work we actually do at SPS, not abstract theory
  • No cost to you — every course is funded by the company
  • A route to grow your career here, whatever role you started in
What that means day to day

Installers, field teams, sales and support staff — all building real technical and AI capability. Practical courses that fit around the working day, and a credentialed path for anyone who wants to take it further as the energy sector goes digital.
