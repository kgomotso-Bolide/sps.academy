Accreditation · How it works

Who stands behind your certificate

SAQA runs South Africa's National Qualifications Framework. QCTO designs and quality-assures occupational qualifications, and accredits the providers allowed to deliver them.

QCTODesigns and quality-assures the qualification
→
SAQARegisters it on the NQF, with its own ID
→
ProviderAccredited to deliver it to you
